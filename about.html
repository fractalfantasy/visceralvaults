About

Visceral Vaults is an independent record label based in Montreal, Québec, releasing collaborative music that moves between trap, R&B and bass — pairing rap vocalists with electronic production.

The catalog includes releases with Gangsta Boo, Just Blaze, Sicko Mobb, King Louie, DVA, Killa P, Zora Jones and Drippin, produced primarily by label founder Sinjin Hawke.
