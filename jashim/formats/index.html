Press kit · five formats

01
One-sheetWhite, editorial: the cover and the player stay put while the release, singles, tracklist and photos scroll by.
 02
Listening roomBlack, music first: one big play button over the cover's blur, the tracklist with a progress line per song.
 03
ZineLight grey paper, serif, pull quotes and full-bleed photos; the album track by track.
 04
DossierA monospace fact sheet that prints to PDF: facts, tracklist, downloads, release, contact.
 05
PosterA screen at a time: each song's name in stretched Impact, fitted to the width; a player bar at the bottom.
